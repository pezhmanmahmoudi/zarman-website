Zarman request pricing and admin redesign — 8 October 2026

IMPLEMENTED
The original amount and currency are sent to the server. Fresh rates, loyalty and promo terms are calculated there, and the chosen Toman budget or AUD principal is retained. The final review and dashboard summary share the same saved quote. Expired quotes cannot be submitted. Public rate/config caches are invalidated after saved finance settings.

Example, buying AUD with an exact 870,000,000 Toman budget, no fee:
182,100 Toman/AUD -> 4,777.59 AUD; currency rounding +861 Toman.
182,600 Toman/AUD -> 4,764.51 AUD; currency rounding +474 Toman.
The funding amount remains exactly 870,000,000 Toman. With a 25 AUD express fee, the fee fits inside the selected budget and payout is 4,739.51 AUD at 182,600.

Admin summary includes a compact pricing box: stored exchange rate, applicable loyalty benefit, promo code and benefit, fees and currency rounding. Rate display retains fractional precision. Transaction tables include customer trade direction and exchange rate. Each row has one blue primary workflow action; email shortcuts and the duplicate final View action were removed. Responsive cards retain labeled fields on narrow screens.

Both linked-request amounts can be edited in one correction with a required reason. The database locks and validates the request and transaction, rejects stale versions, commits quote/transaction/event/audit/command atomically, and retains the original accepted quote. Repeated commands do not duplicate changes. After any payment receipt, recorded deposit, funds or ledger entry, financial amounts are locked. A revision removes previous payment approval and requires the owning customer to explicitly accept the revised amounts. Ordinary replies do not accept financial terms. Cancellation closes pending acceptance without fabricating consent.

Earlier admin recovery changes retain the last confirmed records when a refresh fails, allow scoped read retries, and avoid replaying writes or replacing an entire workspace following routine actions.
